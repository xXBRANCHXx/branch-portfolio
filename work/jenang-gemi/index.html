JENANG GEMI

A modern home and storefront for a traditional Indonesian stomach-health brand, with campaign orders traced back to the channel that brought them in.

The brief

Jenang Gemi makes arrowroot porridge and an herbal drink for sensitive stomachs. Founded in 2017 and acquired by Zero Foods Indonesia in 2024, it needed a more modern voice and a site that sells.

The site had four jobs: explain two products and help people choose, turn visits into WhatsApp orders, recruit resellers and partners, and show which campaigns actually bring in sales.

WHAT I BUILT

  1. 01

    Fifteen pages

    Home, two product pages, two partner-program pages, about and FAQ, plus eight campaign landing pages: one per product for YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok.

  2. 02

    A product-match quiz

    Four questions recommend Bubur or Jamu and explain why, then let you pick a flavor and pack and add it to the cart.

  3. 03

    Cart to WhatsApp

    A slide-out cart and an address step send a ready-made WhatsApp order. It checks live stock first and falls back to plain WhatsApp if the catalog is down.

  4. 04

    Orders traced to campaigns

    Every order from a campaign page carries a code such as YTJGB15OR (YouTube, Bubur, 15 sachets, Original). A WhatsApp Business webhook spots the code in the customer’s message and logs the sale against its source.

  5. 05

    Pages for every affiliate

    Create an affiliate and the system generates a trackable landing page for each affiliate, product and platform.

  6. 06

    Ready for search

    Structured data on every indexed page, a sitemap, an llms.txt file, and a robots file that welcomes AI search crawlers.
