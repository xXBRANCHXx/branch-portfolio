EZKART

A commerce platform for Indonesian sellers: one branded page and one link that goes straight to checkout. I designed and built it on my own.

Role
Solo developer
Scope
Commerce platform · Solo build
Year

The brief

Small Indonesian businesses sell through Instagram bios, ads and WhatsApp chats, and usually stitch together separate tools for their page, payments, orders and shipping. Ezkart puts that into one flow: add a product, design its page without code, share one link.

It’s in early access. The first landing page is free while live payments, payouts and connected shipping are prepared for launch.

WHAT I BUILT

  1. 01

    A bilingual marketing site

    Indonesian by default, with a full English page. The product previews on the page are built in HTML and CSS rather than screenshots, so they stay sharp and light.

  2. 02

    A visual page builder

    A working front-end prototype: drag sections into order, undo and redo, drafts saved in the browser, desktop, tablet and phone previews, and 12 templates.

  3. 03

    A seller admin

    Twelve pages for orders, products, landing pages, customers and more, with a sales chart and a map of orders. It runs on sandbox data until launch.

  4. 04

    A safe checkout loop

    A four-step checkout with Midtrans payments and Biteship shipping quotes, in sandbox. The server owns the catalog so the browser can’t set prices, and every payment notice is signature-checked before an order counts as paid.

  5. 05

    A product film, rendered from code

    A 15-second film built as an HTML timeline and recorded with Playwright and FFmpeg, in both languages, with captions.

  6. 06

    Checked at every size

    Tested at 8 screen widths from 320 to 1920 px, with zero automated accessibility violations in either language.
