ROYAL SYRUP

Positioning and a website for a Yogyakarta syrup supplier that sells to cafés, restaurants and hotels.

Role
Design and development
Scope
Positioning · Website
Year
2026
Live site
royalsyrup.id (opens in a new tab)

The brief

Royal Syrup supplies flavored syrups, drink powders and a maple topping to cafés, restaurants and hotels across Java. The positioning fits in one line: imported quality at a local price, with a flavor for almost anything.

royalsyrup.id makes that case to café owners in their own language, then turns interest into a conversation: browse the catalog, ask a question on WhatsApp, or apply to become a reseller.

WHAT I BUILT

  1. 01

    A one-line position

    The home page opens on local prices with imported quality, and every section after it backs that promise up.

  2. 02

    Flavor range first

    A section on the range, “if you can imagine it, we have the flavor”, that leads straight into the catalog.

  3. 03

    The case for concentration

    The high-concentration formula, explained as a saving: a smaller dose per drink and the same taste every time.

  4. 04

    Proof from partners

    Logos of the hotels and cafés that already pour Royal, placed where a new buyer is deciding.

  5. 05

    A sugar-free series

    Its own section and catalog for the low-calorie line, aimed at cafés building lighter menus, with a word from a loyal customer.

  6. 06

    From interest to WhatsApp

    A reseller sign-up and a contact prompt that both open WhatsApp, plus the store, Instagram and YouTube.
