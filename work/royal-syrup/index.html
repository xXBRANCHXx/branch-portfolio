ROYAL SYRUP

Positioning and a website for a Yogyakarta syrup supplier, plus a phone ordering app for the coffee shops it supplies.

Role
Design and development
Scope
Positioning · Website · Order app
Year
2026
Live site

The brief

Royal Syrup supplies flavored syrups and drink powders to coffee shops, restaurants and hotels. The positioning fits in one line: imported quality at a local price, with a flavor for almost anything.

Coffee shops already order over WhatsApp, so the order app keeps that habit and tidies it up: browse, build a cart, pick a delivery slot and send one clear message.

WHAT I BUILT

  1. 01

    royalsyrup.id

    The brand site leads with one line, “Harga lokal, kualitas impor” (local prices, imported quality), then makes the case with flavor range, concentration and a sugar-free series.

  2. 02

    A phone-first catalog

    More than 80 syrup flavors, sugar-free options and powders, with live search across every tab and swipeable best-seller carousels.

  3. 03

    Real delivery rules

    Weekday and Saturday delivery windows, the daily cutoff, Sundays and Independence Day are built in, so the app only offers slots the team can actually make.

  4. 04

    Checkout in one message

    The cart becomes one structured WhatsApp message: items, sizes, quantities, the coffee shop and the delivery date.

  5. 05

    An order log in Google Sheets

    Each order’s coffee shop name is saved to a Google Sheet through a small serverless proxy that keeps the secret off the page and reports clear errors.

  6. 06

    A darker, premium look

    Black, cream and gold to match Royal’s black bottle labels.
