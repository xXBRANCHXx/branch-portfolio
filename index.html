ABOUT ME

I’m Branch, a developer in Yogyakarta. I learned to build software the practical way: as the only person in marketing, every landing page I wanted to advertise was one I had to build myself.

How I got here

  1. 01

    Graphics guy at ZERO

    ZERO was a very small company then. It took me a while to trust my own taste and make things look good.

  2. 02

    Rebrand, then CMO

    I rebranded ZERO. It’s now the largest zero-calorie syrup brand in Indonesia.

  3. 03

    A marketing team of one

    Ads needed landing pages, so I built them. Then I rebuilt our websites, and then an admin panel to track how they were doing and launch new campaigns.

  4. 04

    Every tool, rebuilt

    One thing led to another, until I’d rebuilt nearly every piece of software the company ran on.

  5. 05

    Solo developer at Ezkart

    Hired to build a commerce platform on my own.

  6. 06

    Now

    A business partner at ZERO in a consulting role, with most of my time spent writing software. I’ve built websites for four companies so far.

How I work

I use AI, but the vision is 100% mine. Every site starts with a clear idea of where it’s going, and I get there in about half the time of a traditional build. Websites are where I started, not where I stop: back ends, admin panels, integrations, and the 3D work I still use today.

I’m ready to start my career as a software engineer, building things I’m proud of.
